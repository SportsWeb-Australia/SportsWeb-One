SportsWeb One — Editor How-To

Edit and publish a site's content — no code, all through the SW1 editor.

Companion to SPORTSWEB-EDITABLE-SITE-SOP.md (which defines the read contract the Builder builds against). Law = SPORTSWEB-WEB-STANDARDS.md (S1–S20) for anything build-related, but this document itself is not a build SOP — see the audience note below.

The flow at a glance

The whole job, in order — same numbers as the steps below. You (or the SW1 manager) do every step in this flow; SW1 automatically handles image upload/processing in step 5.

  1. Sign in — pick the site
  2. Open editor — section cards
  3. Style — pick a look
  4. Edit content — words in cards
  5. Images — frame + upload
  6. Preview — check it
  7. Share — draft link
  8. Publish — goes live

Steps — editing, start to finish

1. Sign in and pick the site

Log in to the SW1 admin (enter your 2‑factor code if prompted). If you manage more than one club or business, choose the one you're working on so the editor points at the right site.

2. Open the website editor

Go to Edit website. Sections are stacked as cards — open one at a time and work through them. Use Preview site → (top right) at any point to open the site in a new tab and check your changes.

3. Pick a style (optional)

Under Website style, choose a look — Heritage, Broadcast, Arena, Classic and its presets. It applies live and saves straight away. News & social below it sets whether the site shows written news, a social feed, or both.

4. Edit the content

Open each card, change the fields, then press its Save button — a small "Saved." confirms it. Leave any field blank to use the built-in default. The cards:

Card What it controls
Homepage hero The big banner: small eyebrow line, title, subtitle (keep it short — 140 chars), the hero image, and an optional background video link.
Club logo & branding Your logo/crest (square, transparent PNG works best).
Brand colours Primary (drives the whole site), secondary, tertiary — pick with the swatch or type a #hex.
President's welcome Name, role, welcome message, sign-off, and portrait photo.
Join / membership CTA The heading + blurb inviting people to get involved.
About your club The opening paragraph and photo on the About page.
Footer Acknowledgement of Country, plus up to three footer logos/flags.
Contact details Email, phone, address, Instagram & Facebook links (used across the site).
Page headings On each page (About, Contact, News…): the banner eyebrow, title and intro line.

Each card saves on its own — you don't lose work moving between them, and you can come back any time.

5. Add or change images

Any image field opens a framing tool — drag to position and set the crop, then confirm. The picture uploads to your site's media automatically and the recommended size is shown under each field (e.g. hero ≈ 1920×1080, logo ≈ 512×512). No files to manage by hand.

Who: you pick + frame; the system uploads & processes. When: whenever a photo needs changing. How: click the image field, drag to set the crop, confirm — it saves itself.

6. See your changes

Edits save to the site immediately. Open Preview site → (or reload the public site) to see them. If a change doesn't show, reload the page.

7. Share a draft for review

Under the publish control, Share draft for review gives you a link (/?preview=…) anyone can open without logging in to view the draft read-only and leave feedback. Use Copy to share it, or Regenerate to kill the old link.

8. Publish

When it's ready, press Publish site and confirm — the status pill flips Draft → Published ✓ and the site goes live to the public. Unpublish takes it back to draft (private) any time.

Who: you. When: when it's ready to go live. How: click Publish site, confirm — the pill flips to Published and the site is live.

✓ This is the switch that makes everything live. Until a site is Published, visitors see only its built-in defaults — your edited content appears the moment you publish.

Managed in their own sections

This editor covers page text, images, branding, style and publishing. These live in their own admin areas, not here:

  • News & events — added as individual posts/events.
  • Sponsors — the sponsor list and tiers.
  • Teams, fixtures & ladder — squads and the match centre.
  • Committee / people — only people marked public appear on the site.

! Remember the gate: editing saves privately. A visitor sees your changes only after the site is Published. A brand-new site shows its built-in default content publicly until you publish the real content.
