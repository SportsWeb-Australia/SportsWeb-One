Standard Operating Procedure · Build from Scratch

Build a new site — content from an old site if there is one, design from anything that inspires it.

For a fresh build (new design, new structure) rather than a like-for-like copy. Two things are decided independently: where the content comes from, and where the design comes from. Works for both club and business sites.

◆
Content and design are separate choices. You can harvest content from an old site and synthesise a brand-new design from three sites the client screenshotted — mix freely. If an old site exists, its earned SEO is still a deliverable — inventory the URLs and 301-map them even on a full redesign.

ELI12 Start here — the whole job, step by step

Written for someone who's never done this — nothing technical. Codey does most of it. A You do this tag marks the few bits that are your job; Codey does this is automatic once you send the prompt.

  1. Gather your two inputs. You do this
    Two things to collect first — no passwords, no coding:
    • The words (content). If there's an old site, just have its web address ready — Codey copies the words and pictures across. No old site? A short brief is fine; Codey marks anything missing as “[client to supply]” rather than making it up.
    • The look (design). Find a couple of websites you like the look of (copy their addresses) or take screenshots of designs that inspire you. Codey builds a fresh look from these — it never copies anyone 1-to-1. See the choices: the two axes ↓.
  2. Open a new chat and add your screenshots. You do this
    Start a fresh Claude Code chat. If you have inspiration screenshots, put them into it — drag them onto the message box (the bar at the bottom where you type), or click the paperclip / attach icon and pick them. Only have website addresses, not screenshots? Skip straight to the next step — you'll type the addresses into the prompt.
    📎
    The message box is the bar at the very bottom of the chat. Drop your screenshots right on it, then go to the next step.
  3. Paste the prompt and press Enter. You do this
    Go to the Kickoff prompt (jump to it ↑) and fill in the blanks first — the two that matter are where the content comes from (old site address or “client brief”) and where the design comes from (the website addresses or “screenshots attached”). Then click Copy, paste it into the chat under your screenshots, and press Enter. That single message tells Codey to do everything below.
  4. Codey studies the top-ranking pages, then builds it better. Codey does this
    This is the important part. Before building, Codey looks up the pages that currently rank at the top of Google for what this business does, sees what they cover, and then builds a more complete, faster, better-organised version — the goal is to out-rank them, not just match them. This is now a must in our standards (S4). Along the way it also copies any old-site words and pictures, and — if there was an old site — saves its Google pages so old links keep working. Just watch; Codey asks you only if a real fact or photo is missing (it never invents one).
  5. Look at the private preview. You do this
    Codey deploys a link only you can see (on Cloudflare or Vercel) and sends it to you. Open it and click around. Something wrong? Tell Codey in plain words (“the logo's too big”, “the phone number is old”). Looks good? Type “yes, go.”
  6. Deploy — and cut over if it replaces a live site. You do this Carson OK if live
    Codey puts the finished site live. Brand-new address, no old site? It just goes live — nothing more to do. Replacing a website that's already live? Codey hands you the exact web-address settings to change (copy-and-paste lines) — and you get Carson's OK before changing them, because flipping the web address is the one truly irreversible bit. What to look for: the address opens the new site, and any old links still land on the matching new page.

CHOOSE The two axes — decided separately You choose Codey builds

Axis 1

Where the content comes from

An old site exists

Harvest it — don't re-key by hand. Render each page in the in-app browser, get_page_text, scroll + sweep the DOM/network for images across all hosts. Mine every real fact into CONTENT-BRIEF.md, then improve it (seek real experience, credentials, process, pricing factors, proof). Gaps → [CLIENT TO SUPPLY].

No old site

Work from the client brief into CONTENT-BRIEF.md; everything unknown is a clearly-marked placeholder. Never fabricate facts, testimonials or stats.

Axis 2

Where the design comes from

  • Replicate one live site — read its CSS (tokens, type, spacing) and rebuild it clean, one direction.
  • Inspiration from N sites — capture what works across several, then synthesise a NEW system; don't copy any one 1:1, never lift a competitor's brand or assets.
  • From screenshots — read the images: sample the palette, read the type feel, layout and mood; ask for the source URL when a still misses hover/gallery states.
  • From the brand — palette from the logo (colour-count the PNG) + collateral; fonts to match the sector tone.
i
Record the derived design system (4–6 colours, 2–3 type roles, a layout concept) in CONTENT-BRIEF.md before building — the same way whatever the design source was.

SHAPE Single-page vs multi-page Your call

Small businesses/trades sometimes want a one-pager. Allowed, deliberately: build the standard sections as anchor blocks on one route, but keep every essential fact in real HTML (not only widgets), use real mailto:/tel: CTAs (S12), and — if it replaces an old multi-URL site — 301 the old server-visible URLs while keeping matching section ids so old fragment links still land (S9). Default to genuine multi-page (S3) where there's enough real content.

STOP Guardrails Codey obeys

!
Never invent content, logos, testimonials, credentials, reviews or stats. Never clone a competitor's brand or site — inspiration is patterns, not assets. Never touch a live domain/DNS without sign-off. If you're replacing a live site, the earned SEO is a first-class deliverable — inventory + 301 it, don't lose it.
