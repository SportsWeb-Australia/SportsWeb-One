01 — What changes

01How Business One differs

Same machinery as SportsWeb One — Astro SSR, database-first with a static fallback, a runtime-neutral loader, the website_status='published' publish gate, a public media bucket. What changes is the content model: business pages and collections, business design language (never fixtures, ladders, president's welcome or “join the club”).

02 — The page set

02Standard business pages

The default multi-page set — drop what a business doesn't need. Every page answers real questions in real HTML (S6), carries per-page SEO + schema (S4/S5), and uses real CTAs (S12).

PageContainsSchema
HomeHero (what you do + area + CTA), services snapshot, why-us / trust, featured work, testimonials, service area, quote CTAWebPage · LocalBusiness
AboutStory, principal / team, years & experience, credentials & licences, valuesAboutPage · Person
ServicesOverview + a section (or page) per service answering the S6 ten questionsService
Our WorkReal case studies (name, location, problem, scope, materials, solution, outcome, photos) + galleryCreativeWork
ReviewsReal testimonials with names (visible content)— no self-serving rating
Contact / QuoteQuote form (real endpoint), phone/email, hours, embedded map + directions, NAP, service areaContactPage
OptionalPricing / Packages · FAQ (genuine Q&A) · genuine Location pagesOffer · FAQPage
Single-page option. Small trades often want a one-pager: the same sections as anchor blocks on one route. Keep every fact in real HTML, use real mailto:/tel: CTAs, and — if it replaces an old multi-URL site — 301 the old server-visible URLs while keeping matching section ids (S9). Default to genuine multi-page where there's enough real content (S3).

03 — The editable model

03Editable content model

Analogous to SW1's key/value + content tables, business-shaped. Final names land with Business One's schema — keep to this shape.

Text & media keys

hero.eyebrow · hero.title · hero.subtitle · hero.image · branding.logo · about.body.0 · about.photo · why.* · contact.email · contact.phone · contact.address · contact.hours · contact.map · footer.* · site.variant · page.{page}.eyebrow/.title/.intro

Collections (kept out of components — S18/S19, so B1 can source them later)

  • services — name, summary, detail, who-for, process, pricing factors, area, image
  • projects — name, location, date, client type, problem, scope, materials, solution, outcome, images, testimonial, related service/location
  • testimonials — name, role/business, quote
  • team — name, role, bio, credentials, photo

Brand colours on the business record via a gated RPC. Publish gate identical to SW1 — nothing is public until website_status='published'.

04 — Building one

04How to build a Business One site

NEW BUILD

Build from scratch

New design, new structure. Content from an old site (if any) and/or design from inspiration sites + screenshots.

→ SPORTSWEB-BUILD-FROM-SCRATCH-SOP.md

LIVE CUTOVER

Migrate a live site

Replace an existing business site without losing earned traffic — baseline → parity → cutover → watch.

→ SPORTSWEB-LIVE-MIGRATION-SOP.md

Local & maps matter most for trades (S7). Keep NAP identical across the site, the schema and the Google Business Profile; set areaServed; embed a map with directions; update the GBP website link on cutover. Only build a location page where real local evidence exists — no thin suburb pages.
Don't skip citations — a big local-SEO lever, especially for trades. A “citation” is the business Name, Address & Phone (NAP) listed consistently on other sites — Google Business Profile, Bing Places, Apple Maps, plus AU directories (True Local, Yellow Pages, Yelp) and trade ones (hipages, Houzz, Airtasker). Google trusts a business more when these match exactly everywhere, so more “near me” customers find it; old or mismatched details quietly cost rankings. Codey preps the exact NAP + directory list; you list the business.

Who you (Codey preps the exact NAP + directory list + a short description) · When at/after go-live · How pick ONE exact NAP, claim Google Business Profile first, then work down the list — the same details, word-for-word, on every listing.

Editable wiring follows SPORTSWEB-EDITABLE-SITE-SOP.md with the business model above instead of the club one. Deploy on Vercel (business playbook) or Cloudflare — provider-neutral (S17).

Guardrails. Never invent content, logos, testimonials, credentials, reviews or stats — record gaps as [CLIENT TO SUPPLY]. Inspiration is patterns, never a competitor's brand or assets. Never touch a live domain/DNS without sign-off. If replacing a live site, its earned SEO is a deliverable — inventory + 301 it.
